🇺🇸 F-1 VISA PROCESS

F-1 Student Visa, Step by Step

Processing typically takes 3–8 weeks. Start early — ideally 4–6 months before your course begins.

1

Receive Your I-20

Once admitted, your university issues the Form I-20 — the foundation of your F-1 application.

2

Pay the SEVIS Fee

Pay the SEVIS I-901 fee (USD 350) online at FMJfee.com and keep your confirmation.

3

Complete the DS-160

File the Online Non-immigrant Visa Application at ceac.state.gov with a recent photo.

4

Schedule Your Interview

Book your visa interview slot at your nearest US embassy or consulate.

5

Attend the Interview

We prep you with mock interviews so you walk in confident and ready.

6

Receive Your Visa & Fly

Collect your passport with visa stamp and start your pre-departure preparation.

F-1 Visa Document Checklist

☑ Valid Passport (6+ months validity) ☑ DS-160 Confirmation Page ☑ Visa Interview Appointment ☑ Financial Proof (Bank Statements) ☑ GRE / GMAT / IELTS Score Cards ☑ Form I-20 from University ☑ SEVIS Fee Receipt ☑ University Offer Letter ☑ Academic Transcripts & Certificates
ELIGIBILITY

Admission Requirements for Indian Students

Requirements vary by program level and university — here's a general overview.

Undergraduate (Bachelor's)

  • • 12th grade completion (any stream), minimum 75–80%
  • • SAT score: 1200–1550 (varies by university tier)
  • • IELTS 6.0–7.0 or TOEFL iBT 80–100
  • • 2–3 Letters of Recommendation
  • • Statement of Purpose / Personal Essay
  • • Financial proof for visa: approx. USD 40,000–75,000

Postgraduate (Master's / MBA)

  • • Bachelor's degree with 60–70%+ (3.0+ GPA equivalent)
  • • GRE 300–320 (MS) or GMAT 600–720 (MBA)
  • • IELTS 6.5–7.5 or TOEFL iBT 90–105
  • • 2–5 years work experience (for MBA programs)
  • • Statement of Purpose + 3 Letters of Recommendation
  • • Financial proof for visa: approx. USD 30,000–60,000/year
